Lyrico uses unmodified FFmpeg 8.1.3 under LGPL 2.1 or later.
Source: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/tree/1041abdc962f4cc4f394aa8de9dc5236c0c3b9e7
The pinned source is in upstream/; CMakeLists.txt and build.sh reproduce the build.
FFmpeg is dynamically linked and the libraries in this directory can be replaced
with ABI-compatible builds. COPYING.LGPLv2.1 and COPYING.GPLv2 are included.
No GPL, version3, nonfree or external codec libraries are enabled.
